## Native behavior

- Blood and white slabs use actual native placement and one shared source-owned
  terrain worker. Mixed private white/blood selectors stay independent. Existing
  source RNG/cursors, TimeStop, clones and whole-coordinator rewind are retained.
- Shared vanilla BlockItem aliases no longer overwrite `Block.asItem` for vanilla
  acacia/birch slabs, coarse dirt or ancient debris. Dirter and Debris keep their
  actual block behavior and complete authored item prototypes.
- Current/old lucky spawners use their actual native BaseSpawner variants; the
  impulse dart is the eighth special-fireball input and creates a real native arrow.
- Money is an immediate RMB consumable. The original snowball was an input-detection
  workaround. Two currency rewards use the player context, ten merchant offers use
  the merchant context, and the actual villager retains its source health, profession,
  stock and death loot. No extra projectile scheduler or rewind state remains.
- The shared logical item index has separate custom/vanilla/union domains, exactly
  one fresh TMT factory ticket, complete source profiles and prebound native
  conditions/functions. Existing random_glack count/max-stack/removal wrappers remain
  separate; legitimate raw zero quantities are preserved. Binding/reload performs
  codec/registry work; individual draws do not rebuild the index or parse JSON.
- Potion payloads retain literal typed components and execute the actual native
  drink listener or native splash/lingering factory. Empty transport defaults do
  not create effect-free ability tickets. Chemical source doses remain native.
- Hylian blocking uses actual BLOCKS_ATTACKS, native delay, arc, bypasses, wear,
  disable cooldown and feedback. Generated carrier food, ranged behavior and
  independent unbreakability remain intact. It is not the Hylian sword aura.
- Native identity defaults normalize Ice/Rocket/Overload and retain the actual
  head-only night-vision and damage-owned dynamite domains.
- Six wisp item keys have no reachable item reader in either legacy namespace.
  They are explicitly inert findings, not invented companion abilities.

TMT retains its native carrier event boundaries: bow/crossbow direct-use groups
run after accepted native shots, carrot/snowball have direct input, and using/food
retain their own source groups. A test must not invent a bow cast without ammunition
or turn apple consumption into an unrelated direct-use group.
