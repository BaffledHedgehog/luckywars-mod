  selectors, original beacon/template geometry and twenty-four native explosions

  no helper creepers. This engine currently has no public item acquisition.

  them, retaining WILD attribution after a child disappears. Captured damage
  preserves its original source position, difficulty rule and death text.

  modifier/type, preserving every value, weight, component and count. Eight
  previously omitted output tables are explicitly packaged. The source output
  closure is thirty-nine tables. Native payload expansion is checked separately.
