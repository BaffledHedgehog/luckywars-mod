## Reused native contract

ImbaNativeVariants already adapts the ordinary authored firework to vanilla held
selection, Crossbow factory/durability and ChargedProjectiles rocket-power queries.
The same helper now recognizes the private TMT firework capability. Selection reads
the existing immutable CustomData slot cache, bounded to seven properties. It does
not copy NBT or retain another global item cache. Ordinary stacks still exit early.

Crossbow factory resolves the selected private firework payload only for its projectile
argument. Actual vanilla draw/debit retains the original physical TMT carrier and
debits it once. The genuine vanilla FireworkRocketEntity constructor, native speed,
shot angle, owner and durability remain authoritative. No copied item events, hand
replacement, fake charging action, second ammo factory or extra ordinary arrow.

Fireworks remain held-only ammunition, matching vanilla; inventory search remains
ARROW_ONLY. Ordinary bows do not accept fireworks. Existing TMT arrow payloads keep
their separate extra-projectile-property contract.
