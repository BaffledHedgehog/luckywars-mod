## Shared conductor and source quirks

- `raycast_electro42` calls `raycast_electro_electroprovod` when the current block is in `#minecraft:electroprovod`, then kills the trace otherwise. The tag includes water (`data/minecraft/tags/block/electroprovod.json`). Conductive callback emits five end-rod particles, then scans mobs within distance 10 except `spectator` and `rcdenyalways`; each invokes the damage helper for 1 generic damage (the helper suppresses damage for `techno_set_bonus_active`). This callback has no owner/team filter and can damage caster/allies.
- Ordinary `raycast_molnia` calls the conductor callback for water and separately for every non-air block, so water satisfies both commands in one sample and gets two callbacks. A ray inside a conductive volume can repeat this at later samples. `electro_ray` checks for air before recursing; solid conductors stop it without the conductor callback.
- Ordinary ray creates three tightly grouped traces; the selector uses `limit=3` to copy rotation and IDs, without explicit sort. Electro meditation quest branch adds 60 full-sphere random rays at the same position. `random_rot_add_active` uses full yaw/pitch ranges, so the fan includes directions behind and vertically downward from the caster.
- Ordinary traces shorten at solid nonconductors. Meditation traces call the conductor check on solid blocks, then die unless the block is conductive; repeated samples within conductor blocks can repeat conduction.
- No separate chain marker/function is called by the staff routes. Ordinary and upgraded mode-1 traces turn as the same marker every ~2 blocks; upgraded mode-2 ray is a separate recursive straight ray, not a chain.
- Direct damage differs by branch: ordinary attempts 0.5 arrow damage against up to two mobs per sample and explicitly marks same `lbcID2`/team first; meditation attempts 0.5 generic damage against each eligible entity in its current cell, with legacy NPC exclusions. The port contract removes allegiance filtering for meditation and credits the meditating player as source. Legacy LBC adds both arrow and generic to bypasses_cooldown: separate damage calls are not suppressed by hurt cooldown. The complete tag is restored in the native port by user clarification on 20 October.

## Visuals and shield/owner metadata

Both trace functions emit `dust_color_transition` from RGB `(0.0,0.8,1.0)` to `(0.0,0.2,0.2)`, scale 0.8, plus `electric_spark`; both play firework twinkle at pitch 2 each sample. Cast preloop also plays twinkle at pitch 1. Ordinary marker tags include `raycastmolnia`, `slowraycast`, `shield_blockable`, `mecha`, `player`; meditation traces include `raycastelectro`, `tuntija`, `slowraycast`, `shield_blockable`, `mecha`. The three ordinary traces receive caster rotation, `lbcID2`, and `team_number`. The 60 meditation traces receive random rotation and `actived`, but these preloop lines do not copy owner ID/team fields. Shield blocking is enabled by the shared `shield_blockable` tag.

## User clarification and quest progress source

The user clarified the intended meaning (quoted verbatim):

> upgradable - это тег после интеракции в академии магов, после которого игрок может выполнять условия улучшения. Затем после выполнения условий он получал другой тег. Эти 60 снарядов во все стороны - это медитация жезла. Эти снаряды не принадлежат никому и наносят урон всем (однако источник урона - игрок который медитирует). Короче медитация это условие для улучшения, а не какой то отдельный режим жезла (но да медитация по сути работает как доп. квестовый режим жезла)

Treat that clarification as the port contract: `upgradable` means eligible to work toward the upgrade after Academy interaction; completion is a later state/flag. The random fan is the meditation quest mode/condition and remains an additional branch of the Electro cast. Legacy fan markers intentionally have no source/team metadata, but legacy `raycast_electro` uses bare `damage @s 0.5 generic` (no `by <player>`); the port should preserve no allegiance filtering while attributing damage to the meditating player.
