# Stblind blind wand: source audit

Read-only audit of the legacy datapack at `E:/servers_mew/cringe1_21_4_skywars/world/datapacksdisabled/LBC`; scope is the ordinary wand and Academy-upgraded wand. The live call chain is datapack `minecraft:tick` → `lbc:tick` → `lbc:tick_not_timestopped` → each `slowraycast` raycaster → `lbc:raycastselector` → `lbc:raycastselector_player` → `lbcr:raycast_blindwand` (or `_phantom`). See `data/minecraft/tags/function/tick.json`, `data/lbc/function/tick.mcfunction`, `tick_not_timestopped.mcfunction`, `raycastselector.mcfunction`, `raycastselector_player.mcfunction`; this establishes normal every-tick processing. The `marker_2t` path is unrelated to these wand rays and is intentionally not used for the speed inference.

## Cost, projectile geometry, and terminal effects

- Cost is 250 mana per successful cast for either ordinary or upgraded wand. `data/lbc/function/config.mcfunction` sets `stblind_wand_mana_use` to 250; `workingitems/sauvojen_staffs/blind.mcfunction` and `blind_upgraded_pre_2.mcfunction` subtract it. Spell entry gates require mana at least that value and reject a magic stopper within 20 (`workingitems/spell1_default.mcfunction`, `spell1_upgraded.mcfunction`, and left-hand counterparts).
- Ordinary mode starts five ray markers at center and ±0.1 on local X/Y. Upgraded default starts seven: those five plus ±0.1 on local forward Z. Upgraded `phantom` mode starts four (center, ±X, +Y). Each marker has `data.lifetime:30`, tags `slowraycast`, `player`, `shield_blockable2`, `magic`; ordinary/default get `raycastblindwand`, phantom gets `raycastblindwand_phantom`. References: `blind_preloop.mcfunction`, `blind_preloop_upgraded_default.mcfunction`, `blind_preloop_upgraded_phantom.mcfunction`.
- On every world tick each projectile ray recursively checks up to six 0.3-block steps (1.8 blocks/tick), then at Distance 7 runs `lbcr:raycast_end_with_tp` which resets Distance to zero. Thus nominal travel while alive is up to 1.8 × 30 = 54 blocks, subject to block collision; the spawned marker is killed when `~ ~ ~` is not air (`lbcr:raycast_blindwand*.mcfunction`). The mode-3 rain tracer uses 0.4 blocks per recursive step, stops at Distance 250 or first same-block mob / non-air block, so max reach ~100 blocks; it repeatedly invokes its tracer function in the same tick (`blind_preloop_upgraded_rain.mcfunction`).
- Main/phantom target acquisition selects mobs at the ray position with `dx=0,dy=0,dz=0,distance=..10`; effectively the one-block selector volume at the projectile location (the 10-block radius is an additional cap), not a swept hitbox. `lbcr:damagewithid7.mcfunction` again applies the same 1-block selection, excluding `nodamage`, `nodamage1`, and spectators before effects. `blind-wand_effects.mcfunction` applies 0.5 generic damage, blindness 10s, wither 4s. `damagewithid7_kill.mcfunction` strips same-owner and same-team targets through `nodamage` / `nodamage1` (and kills the ray marker).
- `shield_blockable2` makes the ray susceptible to `items/shields/shield_block_2.mcfunction`: on a shield block, the scan is centered 1 block ahead of the shield user and sees raycasters within radius 2. Same-owner and same-team projectiles are tagged `donotblock`; other `shield_blockable2` rays are killed only if their current block is air. This differs from `shield_blockable`, whose matching projectiles are killed without the air-block guard. Shield callback is from `items/shields/shield_block.mcfunction`; no explicit projectile hitbox is used for the shield check.
- Direct ray caster attribution is partially present: preloop copies caster `lbcID2` and `team_number` to every marker before activation. Damage executes through `dmgwithid_un_playeronly`; this is the legacy owner/team routing. There is no Java-side source here and the new port must preserve owner attribution for delayed damage per current user contract.

## Upgrades / modes and frequency

- Upgraded wand sneak-right-click changes a persistent mode (1 Default, 2 Phantom, 3 Rain, 4 Gatlingator) and does not cast/spend mana; ordinary right-click invokes selected mode and charges the same 250 once. `blind_upgraded_pre.mcfunction`, `_pre_left`, `_pre_2`; mode-switch functions are `other/magic_academy/change_mode_4*.mcfunction`.
- Default and Phantom create one-shot spreads of 7 and 4 rays per cast; ordinary mode has 5. Rain follows the aim path in same-tick recursive steps, and at the first mob in its selector cell (or solid block) applies blindness 10 seconds to players within 5 blocks, then spawns a `raycastblindwand_cloud` marker 10 blocks above with lifetime 200. Its cloud marker has `marker_2t`, so the actual scheduler is `minecraft:tick` → `lbc:tick` plus the independently self-scheduled `lbc:tick2t` every 2 ticks → `lbc:markers_2t` → `markers_2t1`; it creates ONE ordinary `raycastblindwand` projectile every 2 ticks during its 200-tick lifetime (about 100 projectiles). `universal_pos_razb_10.mcfunction` randomizes its world X and Z coordinates independently by integer offsets -10..10; this is a position scatter, not ten rays. The primary projectiles live 40 ticks. References: `data/lbc/function/tick.mcfunction`, `tick2t.mcfunction`, `markers_2t.mcfunction`, `markers_2t1.mcfunction`, plus the wand functions. Gatlingator adds 20 to `gatling_fovos`; `players_2t.mcfunction` consumes one every 2 ticks via `blind_loop_gatling.mcfunction`, so the burst is 20 rays over 40 ticks (mode selection itself does not spend mana).
- Academy interaction routes a selected item carrying `stblind:1` through `other/magic_academy/can_upgrade/stblind.mcfunction` and `upgrade/stblind.mcfunction`; success modifies mainhand with tier B. The `can_upgrade/stblind` function only prints the localized prompt. During casting, ordinary preloop marks spawned projectiles `check_distance` only when the currently selected mainhand item has `custom_data.upgradable:1` (`blind_preloop.mcfunction`); `blind_preloop_upgraded` does not add this tag. For tagged rays, `lbcr:damagewithid7.mcfunction` invokes `damagewithid7_check_blinded` before damage. That check selects eligible mobs with `dx=dy=dz=0` and `distance=..15` (so still the one-block volume at the projectile, capped at 15), excludes `nodamage`, spectator-tagged, and already-blind entities, then requires an online non-spectator holding `{upgradable:1,stblind:1}` whose `lbcID2` matches the ray owner. `lbc:chance3` is 3% per qualifying callback. It calls general `can_upgrade`, which routes through `can_upgrade_book`: that function sets `upgrade_available:1`, clears `upgradable` to 0, plays the ready sound, and sends the completion message. The separate `can_upgrade/stblind` function only prints the trial instruction. RP `upgrade_condition_stblind` asks the player to shoot mobs or players; both are included by `#minecraft:mobs`. The code checks one block volume at a time, with a 3% chance per qualifying event, rather than maintaining a cumulative target counter. Also, a left-hand cast goes through `blind_preloop`, whose `SelectedItem` condition reads mainhand, so `check_distance` may depend on the other hand item.

## Port questions / hazards

- Ported Rain uses the native first-hit path; its nearby player blindness uses the shared owner/alliance/observer target guard. Its cloud emits one falling projectile every 2 ticks for up to 200 ticks. The old datapack `tag=!caster` radius effect is not copied verbatim; native hit and team guards govern the port.

- Legacy target damage is capped at 0.5 generic per effect callback and then applies blindness+wither; preserve damage ownership and same-team filtering through native attribution/ally checks. `nodamage` cleanup is global in `damagewithid7_kill.mcfunction`, a legacy state-coupling risk.

## Сверка с текущим переносом (read-only)

Исходный `minecraft:entity_type/mobs.json` включает `minecraft:player`; blind callbacks по `type=#minecraft:mobs` рассматривают игроков, если их не исключает другой селектор/тег. Обычный upgraded-default исходник создаёт именно 7 лучей: исходный центр и ±X/±Y плюс ±Z; Phantom создаёт 4. В `LbcBlind.cast` соответствуют ordinary=5, upgraded default=7, Phantom=4.

Лут-импорт `--blind` сохраняет исходные имя, model, tier, структуру и стили lore, а также исходный custom data. Academy JSON добавляет `{upgradable:0,upgrade_available:1,upgraded:1,mode:1}`, затем исходные `add_upgrade_desc` и `tiers/b`: зелёная некурсивная строка `upgraded_item` и tier modifier. По прочитанным loot JSON и item_modifier расхождений форматирования не найдено.

Интеграция переноса: `LbcWands` списывает ману и переключает режим Academy-жезла; `LbcBlind` живёт в `LbcItems.tick/stop`. Стандартный попадание создаёт авторизованный `DamageSource` с `charge` и владельцем, проверяет цель через общий wand target, затем наносит 0,5 generic, blindness 200 тиков и wither 80. Trial ограничен реальным новым попаданием и 3%; результат применяет `LbcAcademy.ready` к исходной руке, пока там остаётся тот же тип испытательного жезла.

Shield mismatch на Phantom: legacy `shield_block_2` выбирает лучи в радиусе 2 вокруг точки перед щитом, но убивает `shield_blockable2` только если текущий блок луча — `#minecraft:airs`. Перенос пропускает shield check для Phantom, если блок в `from` не air; однако при сегменте, начинающемся в воздухе и пересекающем стену, `shieldHit(from,to)` может вернуть точку внутри стены. Если нужно точное legacy-поведение, проверять блок именно в returned shield-hit position, а не только в начале сегмента. В shared `LbcWands.target` нет явного фильтра отдельной роли shop/NPC; исходный entity-type tag включает villagers и traders, поэтому вопрос продавца относится к общему фильтру целей.

## Краткая сверка текущей реализации

Проверен код `LbcBlind` и вызовы в `LbcWands`, `LbcAcademy`, `LbcItems`: ordinary создаёт 5 нативных `Charge`, Academy default — 7 (центр, ±lateral, ±vertical, ±forward), Phantom — 4. Каждый снаряд раз в игровой тик проверяет swept-сегмент длиной 1,8 блока; первый hit завершает полёт, иначе срок — 30 тиков. Academy trial вызывается при новом попадании в ранее не ослеплённую цель; шанс 3% вызывает `ready` и ставит `upgrade_available`. Rain использует первый native hit на пути до 100 блоков и создаёт по одной падающей Charge каждые 2 тика на срок cloud до 200 тиков; Gatling создаёт до 20 Charges, по одной каждые 2 тика. `LbcItems.tick/stop` обслуживают и очищают состояние жезла. Это сверка кода; тестовый запуск и ответ клиента не проверялись.
