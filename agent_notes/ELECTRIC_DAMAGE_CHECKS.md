# Electric damage checks

`GameChecks` now provides `electric-damage-menu`, `electric-damage`, and `electric-damage-cleanup`. The behavior group calls `hurtServer` with registry-backed `LbcDamage.ELECTRIC` and the two legacy alias keys. It checks repeated electric hits against the ordinary native cooldown control, the additive raw bonus for one through four equipped pieces across copper, iron, chainmail, golden, and netherite armor, and native `minecraft:player_attack` oracle hits through the same armor, toughness, Protection, Resistance, and absorption pipeline. The checks assert both oracle sources do not bypass armor and verify the protection fixture reduces damage below raw input. They also cover unrelated vanilla custom data that must retain the bonus, the `multiplying`, `randchanted`, and Mage legacy custom-set markers, native Polymer rocket boots, mixed full Techno/Technomk2 immunity for all aliases, partial-set damage, velocity preservation for players and mobs, ordinary knockback, alias bonus/knockback behavior, and observer damage exclusion. Player state uses the existing `WandThirdPlayerState` fixture and restoration path.

`tools/smoke_lbc_loadouts.py --electric-damage-only` runs the three groups around one match. The full LBC smoke flow adds the same groups after the Electro wand slice, for an expected total of 78 groups across 26 matches.

The root agent ran the focused smoke successfully in 51.41 seconds: one match, all three electric groups plus the common bot check passed. Full regression passed in 382.70 seconds: 26 matches, 74 completed scenarios and 78 unique check groups.
